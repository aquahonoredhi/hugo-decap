  As the temperature starts to fall and the leaves follow suit, the season of
  rainy days is just beginning and what better way is there to spend such days
  then curled up inside with a good book?  

  However, we all know trying to find a good book is no joke especially at this time of year with all the sports and school, where to start, what book to pick? You barely have time to finish your homework, you don’t have time to go find another book. No worries we got you! Grab your coffee and a pen ‘cause you’ll want to write these down. Here are our top ten book recommendations for the fall this year:
---
1. *Trust Fall* by Lynn Painter - From the author of our beloved *Better Than the Movies*, Lynn Painter once more gives us an enemies-to-lovers rom-com in the form of *Trust Fall*. In her newest novel a small town girl inherits a fortune and moves to Manhattan where she is thrown into the life of an heir in which she meets a mysterious stranger…  
2. *The Third Gilmore Girl* by Kelly Bishop and *Have I Told You This Already?* by Lauren Graham - For fans of Gilmore Girls (everyone’s favorite comfort show) these memoirs are must reads. Whether you’re in the mood for an elegant read from Emily Gilmore herself or want some of Lorelai’s lighthearted and humorous reflections you’ll be sure to find them and learn more about Gilmore Girls from behind the scenes and the actors backgrounds as rising stars in the film industry. 
3. *A Good Girl’s Guide to Murder* by Holly Jackson - Check out this thrilling murder mystery! A girl reopens a closed murder case in her small town for her final school project but the further her investigation goes the closer her own life is to danger. 
4. *The Raven Boys* by Maggie Stiefvater - A girl raised by a family of psychics befriends four prep school students known as the “raven boys”. Be sure to check it out if you’re in for a dangerous and mysterious quest. 
5. *Anne of Green Gables* by L.M. Montgomery - Into the classics? What better book to read in the fall than Anne of Green Gables? Anne herself’s favorite season is October and the cozy and nostalgic setting is sure to make this book your new comfort read. 
6. *Pumpkinheads* by Rainbow Rowell, illustrated by Faith Erin Hicks - If you need an easy read and enjoy beautiful illustrations this book is for you! Get lost in Pumpkinheads, a graphic novel that explores the bittersweet reality of growing up and long standing friendships in an autumn carnival. 
7. *Miss Peregrine’s Home for Peculiar Children* by Ransom Riggs - A story told in a mix of gripping narrative and black and white photography. This eerie and vintage novel is a great way to start off the spooky season. 
8. *Better Than Revenge* by Kasie West - If your favorite fall activity is going to football games and the drama of a new school year you're in luck. Better Than Revenge captures the feeling of needing to achieve payback for your dreams in the form of a contemporary romance. 
9. *Immortal Consequences* by I.V. Marie  - As a beautiful novel perfect for fantasy readers the fast paced book follows six students competing in a magical academy caught between the afterlife and purgatory. 
10. *Carrie* by Stephen King - The debut novel of the “king of horror” himself gives us a perfect novel about a bullied teenager with telepathic abilities. ( if you can take a scare…)

 \*Bonus Librarian’s Pick\* - *In The Blood* by April Henry - This new addition to the Talawanda High School library will be sure to fit right into your October reading list as this suspenseful read follows an adopted girl whose DNA test turns out to connect her to a serial killer father.
